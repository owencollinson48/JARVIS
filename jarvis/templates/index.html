Voice & accessibility

Browser speech can use browser/OS services. Consent is requested before voice starts. JARVIS does not record or upload microphone audio to its backend. Recognition is browser-dependent; text input always remains available. Speaking animation follows speech events, not measured output volume.

Enable browser voice?

Speech recognition and speech output may send audio or text to your browser or operating system provider. Support varies by browser. Use current Edge or Chrome on Windows for the best chance of recognition support.

JARVIS does not retain raw audio or send it to its backend. A transcript appears as a draft. Review it and choose Send before any AI request or memory command runs. Existing API costs apply only when you submit an AI request.
